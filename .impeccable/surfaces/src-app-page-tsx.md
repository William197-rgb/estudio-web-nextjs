## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a web studio, played straight at Stripe's finish and Framer's structure: the visitor sees the product (real example sites on real devices) before any adjective. It refuses the old night-and-neon look and any costume world.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, one tone step (#F4F6F5) for alternating bands, one deep-green ink family (#0E1A16) for text and the dark bands, one accent green (#0A7D55) reserved for actions. Schibsted Grotesk only, heavy and tight for display, regular for text. Pill buttons, 14px device frames with a soft offset shadow, no rules, no gradients, no eyebrows, no mono. Recorded 2026-10-27 after the finish review: the WhatsApp chat preview counts as a device frame and keeps its float shadow, and a clickable example card lifts with the same shadow on hover. The accent green marks actions and progress: buttons and links, the process step dots and the price checkmarks; plain text never takes it.

STORY: A business owner understands in one screen that William builds phone-first sites that keep answering on WhatsApp after hours, sees one for his own trade, watches an ad made with his name, checks the price, and sends the WhatsApp message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: h1 "Su local cierra. Su web sigue atendiendo." across all 12 columns at up to 6rem, two lines from tablet up (in a half-width column the second line needs 3 to 5 lines, so the full width is the only way it holds two). Below it, a left column with the lede, primary green pill "Pedir mi propuesta" and secondary "Ver ejemplos", then the live Asunción clock line; a right column with a laptop browser frame showing the dentist example site and a phone frame overlapping its lower right (so it never covers the laptop site's headline) with the vet example, whose status reads open or closed from the real clock while its WhatsApp button stays live. On phones the devices follow the actions directly and the clock line moves below them. Header: wordmark, five links, WhatsApp pill.

FORM: Canon (the standing exit), chosen by the user over the dealt hand; no position on the grounded list. seed:7bd0a0e02bf72cb6. Signature interaction: the phone mock follows Asunción time (open/closed) while its WhatsApp stays on. Motion grammar: one hero entrance of the devices, staggered heading-text-media reveals once per section, the pinned rebuild story, the process line drawing, the score counters.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
